06 — Transformation Playbook

How I Deliver Change as an FD

1

Diagnose

  • Identify where value is created and lost.
  • Focus on commercial drivers, not just financial outputs.
2

Prioritise

  • Target high-impact areas (margin, cash, ROI).
  • Avoid over-engineering.
3

Embed

  • Integrate finance into operations.
  • Align teams around clear performance metrics.
4

Scale

  • Implement systems, processes, and governance.
  • Ensure sustainability beyond individuals.
“Transformation is not a project — it's a shift in how the business operates.”
