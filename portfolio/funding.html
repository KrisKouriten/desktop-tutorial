04 — Funding & Cash Strategy

Enabling Growth Without Losing Control

Challenge

  • Rapid expansion required capital ahead of cash generation.
  • Need to balance growth with liquidity risk.

Actions

  • Structured and managed multi-million-pound banking facilities (HSBC).
  • Built rolling cash flow forecasting with scenario planning.
  • Implemented working capital discipline across inventory and payables.
  • Created visibility on the cash impact of expansion decisions.

Approach

  • Linked store rollout plans directly to cash and funding capacity.
  • Ensured growth decisions were financially executable, not theoretical.

Outcome

  • Successfully funded rapid expansion.
  • Maintained liquidity stability during the high-growth phase.
  • Built strong banking relationships and credibility.
“Growth only matters if it can be funded and sustained.”
