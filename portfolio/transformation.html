05 — AI & Finance Transformation

Moving Finance from Reporting to Real-Time Decision Support

Current Reality (Most Businesses)

  • Retrospective reporting cycles.
  • Heavy manual processes.
  • Insight delivered too late to influence outcomes.

My Perspective

Finance is moving toward:

  • Real-time performance visibility.
  • Predictive, not historical, insight.
  • Embedded decision support within operations.

Practical Applications I Am Driving / Exploring

  • AI-assisted forecasting (sales, store performance, demand).
  • Automated margin and SKU-level analytics.
  • Faster scenario modelling for expansion decisions.
  • Enhanced cash forecasting accuracy using data patterns.

Impact

  • Faster, better-informed decisions.
  • Reduced reliance on manual processes.
  • Finance becomes a commercial co-pilot to the business.
“AI will not replace finance — but it will redefine what good finance looks like.”
