05 — AI & Finance Transformation

Moving Finance from Reporting to Real-Time Decision Support

Current Reality (Most Businesses)

  • Retrospective reporting cycles.
  • Heavy manual processes.
  • Insight delivered too late to influence outcomes.

Where I'm Focused

  • Automating month-end close and management reporting.
  • AI-assisted variance analysis and narrative commentary.
  • Forward-looking, scenario-driven dashboards.
  • Natural-language query layers over finance data.

Why It Matters

  • Finance moves from month-end retrospective to real-time decision support.
  • Faster, better-evidenced commercial decisions.
  • Team capacity redirected from processing to partnering the business.
“The next generation of finance leaders will not just use AI — they will design how the business decides with it.”
