02 — Case Study

Scaling a Retail Platform

Miniso UK

5 → 60
Stores
£9m → £50m+
Revenue
Multi-£m
Banking facilities structured

Context

  • Early-stage UK retail footprint with aggressive growth ambition.
  • Limited infrastructure to support scale.
  • Capital constraints alongside expansion.

My Role

  • Finance lead supporting CEO and board through rapid expansion.
  • Built the financial framework to enable disciplined, scalable growth.

Key Actions

  • Developed store-level ROI models to drive site selection.
  • Implemented capex governance and prioritisation discipline.
  • Built scalable reporting and performance tracking across the estate.
  • Strengthened cash flow management during the high-growth phase.
  • Led banking relationships and funding strategy.

Outcome

  • Growth from 5 to 60 stores.
  • Revenue scale from £9m to £50m+.
  • Maintained financial control while accelerating expansion.
“Growth was not just delivered — it was structured, funded, and controlled.”
