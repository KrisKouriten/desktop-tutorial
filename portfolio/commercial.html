03 — Commercial Decision-Making

Driving Outcomes, Not Just Reporting

Example: Store Portfolio Optimisation

Problem

Inconsistent store performance across the estate, with margin dilution risk.

Approach

  • Built store-level contribution models (beyond headline EBITDA).
  • Segmented the estate into:
    • High-return growth stores
    • Stable performers
    • Underperforming / value-destructive sites
  • Introduced a data-led decision framework: retain / invest / exit.

Decision

  • Redirect capital towards high-return locations.
  • Rationalise underperforming stores.
  • Refocus management attention on key drivers: footfall, conversion, basket.

Impact

  • Improved portfolio-level profitability.
  • Increased return on invested capital.
  • Shifted business mindset from growth at all cost to disciplined growth.
“Finance acted as a decision engine, not a reporting function.”
